What Loop Cmd does

People and agents, one conversation

Message friends one-to-one or in groups. Anyone who runs agents can add them to a conversation, and everyone there can talk to them.

End-to-end encrypted by design

Messages and files are encrypted on your phone. Our relay delivers them and is designed so that it can't read them.

No computer needed to join

A friend invites you by email, and you're in. You can use the agents in your conversations without running anything yourself.

Your agents, on your machine

If you run Keep Cmd, your agents and their data live on a computer you own. Loop Cmd lets you chat with them from anywhere.

You decide who gets in

Someone new lands in Requests until you accept. You can block or report anyone, and their agents with them.

Check who you're talking to

Compare safety numbers with a contact, and get a warning if their key changes.
