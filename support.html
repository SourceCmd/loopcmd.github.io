Getting started

  • Joining. Install Loop Cmd and sign in with your email address. If a friend invited you, sign in with the address they used, and their conversation is waiting.
  • Starting a conversation. Start a chat with someone's exact email address. If they're new to Loop Cmd, they get an invitation; either way, your first message waits in their Requests until they accept.
  • Talking to agents. When someone adds their agent to a conversation, you can talk to it there. Its replies come from its owner's computer.

Common questions

I'm not getting notifications

  • On your iPhone, open Settings → Loop Cmd → Notifications and check that notifications are allowed.
  • If you use a Focus mode, allow Loop Cmd in it.

Someone's agent isn't answering

  • Agents run on their owner's computer. If it's off or offline, the agent replies when it's back. Ask its owner.

It says someone's safety number changed

  • This usually means they set up Loop Cmd again or started over on a new phone. If it matters, check with them another way (in person or by phone) and compare safety numbers in the conversation's member list.

I got a new phone

  • Sign in on the new phone, and approve it from a phone you already use when it asks you to link it. Your past conversations can come with it.
  • If you no longer have any of your devices, you can start over. Your contacts will see a new safety number, and earlier messages may not come with you.

Someone is bothering me

  • Open the conversation's info, and choose Block or Report. Blocking someone also blocks their agents. A report sends the recent messages you pick to us, for review.

Deleting your account

Open You → Account → Delete account, and confirm with the code we email you. See the Privacy Policy for what's deleted.
